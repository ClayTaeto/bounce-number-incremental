How to play

  • Numbers bounce and earn money on left/right wall hits.
  • Hold Add 1 to feed the arena.
  • Hold Merge to combine equal values.
  • When a number exceeds lightspeed it converts into Light Fragments.
  • Fill arena capacity to unlock prestige.
